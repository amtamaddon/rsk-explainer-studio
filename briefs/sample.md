# Sample brief: Why is the night sky dark?

Length: about 2 minutes.
Audience: curious adults, no physics background.
Thesis: In an infinite, eternal, unchanging universe every line of sight would end on the surface of a star, so the night sky should blaze. It is dark because the universe has a finite age, light has a finite speed, and expansion stretches the most distant light out of the visible band.
Must include: one Manim diagram of sight lines meeting stars in nested shells; one generated shot of a night sky over a field; one Creative Commons clip of an observatory if one exists.
Tone: warm, unhurried, professorial.
Budget: stop and ask if video generation spend passes $25.
