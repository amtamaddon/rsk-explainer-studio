# Brief: SCALE prep series (four parts)

Source: RSK field guide "Payment variance, payer contracts and the MSO world", prepared September 10, 2026, for the SCALE Healthcare Leadership Summit, October 13 to 14, New York.
Audience: one viewer from the operations side, new to healthcare, attending SCALE to run discovery conversations.
Goal: the viewer already knows underpayment recovery. After four videos they can follow any hallway conversation about the wider industry: who pays, who provides and owns, how prices and value-based care work, and the 2026 news, with every acronym defined on screen as it is spoken.
Length: about 4 to 5 minutes per part.
Parts: 1 Who pays (work/scale-1-payers). 2 Who provides, who owns (work/scale-2-providers). 3 The money and value-based care (work/scale-3-money). 4 What everyone is talking about in 2026 (work/scale-4-news).
Visuals: Manim diagrams and type for every mechanism; one or two generated shots per part (openers and closers only). No real logos, no real people, no RSK product screens.
Accuracy: use only figures in the field guide, said with their source. RSK's 1 to 3% range is a working assumption, never a result.
Tone: warm, unhurried, professorial.
Budget: stop and ask if video generation spend passes $25 across the series.
