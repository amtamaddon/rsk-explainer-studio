# RSK Health: what we do (90-second follow-up video)

One idea: some insurer payments quietly come up short of what the contract promised, and RSK Health finds them, collects them, and helps fix the contract, paid only from what it recovers.

## p01 [warm, welcoming]
Your team posts insurer payments every day, and most of them match the contract. Some come in quietly short, and because the claim shows as paid, nobody has time to go back and check it against the rate you negotiated.

## p02 [steady, clear]
RSK Health starts by finding them. Our platform reads your 835 files and prices every claim line against the payer contracts that govern it, so each underpayment is tied to a specific clause and a specific line on the remittance.

## p03 [steady, clear]
Then we collect. Our team takes each variance to the payer with an evidence package built to hold up, works through reconsiderations and appeals, and keeps every filing deadline in view until the money is posted to your account.

## p04 [thoughtful]
Finally, the same file that proved the underpayments becomes your leverage at renewal, and we help you renegotiate the payer contract so its terms work for you from then on.

## p05 [warm, closing]
Whether you run urgent care clinics or a behavioral health group, there is no software to buy, we work from the files you already have, and our fee is a contingency paid only from what we recover. Let us run a look-back on your data. If we find nothing, we'll tell you so, and you won't owe us anything.
